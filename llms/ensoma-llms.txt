> Ensoma is a privately held, clinical-stage genomic medicines company developing in vivo
> hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) engineering. Its platform pairs an off-the-shelf in vivo
> delivery system built on high-capacity virus-like particles that preferentially bind HSCs
> with a gene engineering toolkit spanning base editing and high-efficiency gene integration,
> carrying payloads of roughly 35 kilobases. Ensoma publishes no public developer API,
> developer portal, or machine-readable API contract.
